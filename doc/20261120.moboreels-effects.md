# MoboReels social effects

The report previously queried clicks and revenue with separate date ranges and
only refreshed money after observing a running-to-success sync transition.
`/moboreels-report.html` now uses one filter, up to three grouping dimensions,
six KPIs, daily trends, rankings, sortable detail and full-result CSV. Successful
sync timestamp changes and tab restoration refresh the report.

`GET /api/moboreels/reports?view=effects` retains the existing cookie permission
and institution/admin versus owner scope. It reuses the decimal financial
aggregation at promotion/day grain and joins clicks by immutable short code.
Shared promotion revenue is retained once in shared-channel/account buckets.
Filters exclude unallocatable revenue from individual channels and disclose the
excluded amount. Clicks use Beijing dates; money uses unconverted supplier dates.
Coverage distinguishes completed empty snapshots from unsynced days. No schema,
cron, upstream request parameters or publishing behavior changes.

Validation: 27 existing order tests; 36 service/click/bridge/effects tests;
`node tests/test_moboreels_effects_ui.js` covers auth, refresh without observing
running status, stale responses, permission revocation and exact CSV values.
